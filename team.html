About us

Small enough to stay close. Experienced enough to go deep.

Dualog is the shared studio of Emma and Julia. We combine strategy, identity and digital design in one close process — from the first idea to the final detail.

    Emma and Julia begin working together across identity and editorial design.

  3. 2024

  4. 2025

    Berlin — worldwide

    Two designers, one studio, collaborating with ambitious teams wherever they are.
